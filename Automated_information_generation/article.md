### 4. The Workforce Evolution: Augmentation Over Replacement
Contrary to early fears of a "jobs apocalypse," recent data from the *Business Roundtable* and the *Burning Glass Institute* suggests that AI is **changing jobs for the better** rather than eliminating them. The technology is increasingly being used to **complement human labor**, automating routine tasks to allow workers to focus on **judgment, creativity, and complex problem-solving.**

In the enterprise sector, companies like Genpack are leading the way by rolling out **"agentic products"** where humans and AI agents work collaboratively. This "human-plus-agent" workflow is proving to be a powerful driver of **productivity and corporate profit**, particularly in finance and supply chain operations.
